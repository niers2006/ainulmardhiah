📚 My Education Journey

Primary School

I studied at SK Sri Suria 1, Tanah Merah, Kelantan from Year 1 until Year 6. During my primary school years, I learned many basic subjects such as Malay, English, Mathematics and Science. I also learned how to be more disciplined and responsible with my studies. Also, my favourite subject at that time is History beacuse it give me a lot of information about what happen to our country before Independent Day of Malaysia.

At the end of primary school, I took the UPSR examination and got 2A, 2B and 2C. This result motivated me to work harder in the future and improve myself in secondary school.

Secondary School

I continued my studies at Maahad Ahmadi, Tanah Merah, Kelantan from Form 1 until Form 5. In this school, I learned both academic and religious subjects which helped me become more balanced in my education and daily life.

In Form 4, I took SMU and achieved 8A out of 10. In Form 5, I took SPM and got 5A. This experience taught me about discipline, time management and consistency in studies.

University

Currently, I am studying at Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM) Cawangan Kedah in Library Science. This course teaches me how information and libraries are managed professionally.

So far, I have achieved Dean’s List from Semester 1 until Semester 3. I hope to continue improving and graduate successfully in the future.
