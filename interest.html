My Interests 💖

🎵 Music

One of my main interests is music, especially K-pop. I enjoy listening to different music genres, but K-pop is my favourite because of its catchy songs, meaningful lyrics and energetic vibes. My favourite song is “Joyride” by CORTIS. The reason I love music is because it helps me release stress, especially when I am doing work or studying.

🎬 Movies

I like watching movies, especially fantasy like Harry Potter. I love Harry Potter because the story is magical, interesting and full of adventure. Each movie shows character growth, friendship and courage.

The movie makes me feel like I am inside a magical world. It also teaches many life values like bravery, loyalty and teamwork. I enjoy rewatching it because it never gets boring.

🏸 Sports - Badminton

Another interest of mine is badminton. I enjoy playing badminton because it is fun, active and helps me stay healthy. It improves my reflexes, focus and physical strength.

Besides that, badminton also teaches discipline and teamwork. I often play with friends and family.
